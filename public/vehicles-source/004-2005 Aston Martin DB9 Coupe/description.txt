  - Designed by Ian Callum and Henrik Fisker, the DB9 made its debut for 2005 to replace the DB7. It was built on a new aluminum-intensive platform and it inaugurated a thoroughly revised version of the firm's 5.9-liter V12. It was available as both a 2+2 coupe or convertible, and it handled markedly better than its predecessor thanks in part to a 50/50 weight distribution achieved by moving the engine back in the bay and fitting a rear-mounted transaxle.
  - Power comes from a 5.9-liter V12, rated at 450 horsepower and 420 lb-ft of torque. Output is sent to the rear wheels via a 6-speed automatic transmission.
